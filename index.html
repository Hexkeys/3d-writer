0.0 mm · pen tip aligned with nozzle reference

At 0 mm, the simulated pen tip and nozzle tip share the same height reference. Moving this control moves the whole toolhead and attached pen together; it does not actuate the real holder.

Dimension scale: 1:1 (1 scene unit = 1 mm). Published A1 body envelope: 465 × 410 × 430 mm; build volume: 256 × 256 × 256 mm. The frame and moving parts are a dimensionally scaled reconstruction, not manufacturer CAD. The pen is fixed beside the nozzle and moves with the complete toolhead; the 0 mm calibration position aligns the simulated pen tip with the nozzle tip. The simulator is visual only and does not control your printer.

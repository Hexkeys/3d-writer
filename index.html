Before you run it

This creates generic Marlin-style G-code: X/Y moves the printer toolhead with the attached pen, and Z lifts or lowers the pen. The paper is assumed to remain fixed on a 256 × 256 mm plate. Actual motion depends on your printer mechanics; verify the coordinate system and test with the pen lifted before drawing. Calibrate pen Z and offsets for your holder.

05 / MACHINE SIMULATOR

Bambu Lab A1-style playback

Waiting for G-code
▦Generate G-code to build the 3D toolpathDrag to orbit · Scroll to zoom

The pen is rigidly fixed to the side of the toolhead and has no separate actuator. Before travel, Z raises the complete A1-style gantry and head so the pen clears the clipped paper; the gantry lowers to make contact for drawing. X moves the head and Y moves the bed and paper. This is an illustrative model, not an exact mechanical replica, and does not control your printer.
